About Me 关于我

I’m an M.S. student in Cyberspace Security at Guizhou Normal University. I earned my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing Tech University. I’m interested in how software and AI systems can be designed to behave reliably when they process untrusted data or act on incomplete information.

我目前在贵州师范大学攻读网络空间安全硕士学位，本科毕业于南京工业大学计算机科学与技术专业。我关注如何让软件和人工智能系统在处理不可信数据或依据不完整信息采取行动时，仍能保持可靠。

My project experience includes cryptographic engineering for transportation billing and work on multi-agent tools for security investigations. These projects have drawn my attention to two practical questions: how to establish trust in the data a system uses, and how to keep track of the evidence behind an investigation’s conclusions.

我参与过交通计费场景的密码工程，也参与过面向安全调查的多智能体工具开发。这些经历让我关注两个实际问题：如何判断系统所用的数据是否可信，以及如何追溯安全调查结论所依据的证据。

Research Interests 研究兴趣

  • AI Security 人工智能安全

    How AI systems respond to untrusted inputs and how to evaluate the reliability of their outputs.

    关注人工智能系统如何应对不可信输入，以及如何评估输出的可靠性。

  • Agent Security 智能体安全

    How to constrain what agents can do with tools and review their decisions in multi-step workflows.

    关注如何约束智能体调用工具的权限，以及如何审查它们在多步骤流程中的决策。

  • Software Security 软件安全

    How to identify security weaknesses in software and assess whether fixes address their underlying causes.

    关注如何识别软件中的安全缺陷，并判断修复是否解决了根本原因。

  • Trustworthy Systems 可信系统

    How cryptographic verification and data provenance help systems rely on information that can be checked.

    关注密码验证与数据溯源如何帮助系统使用可核验的信息。
